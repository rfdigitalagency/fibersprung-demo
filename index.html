How a floor gets made

Five steps, every time.

  1. 01

    Design and planning

    All FiberSprung floor designs are based in Computer Aided Design. Floors are manufactured from the CAD files to quality tolerances defined by drawings, ensuring consistency across every part. Pre-planned layouts mean lean efficiency, less waste, and cost savings in time and materials.

  2. 02

    Pre-assembly and delivery

    A ~6 week lead time begins once an order is placed. Fiberglass components are built and pre-assembled with FiberSprung fittings, and every piece of plywood and fiberglass joint is CNC machined for a consistently perfect fit. Every quotation includes installation — our own installers travel to your site.

  3. 03

    The fiberglass grid

    The basket-weave support structure goes down first. This is where the spring comes from, and where the 20 year warranty applies.

  4. 04

    The support surface

    A single layer of 3/4" or 1" plywood. Every joint is tongue-and-grooved, then fastened on both sides to a fiberglass plate — supporting the joint from beneath and producing a homogeneous-feeling surface. Single layer construction is a deliberate choice: it makes repair fast when a floor is damaged.

  5. 05

    The performance surface

    Typically marley for dance, with thin engineered hardwood and vinyl also available. Installing the surface is the finishing touch of our service.

Our story

It started with a sprinkler.

The idea for FiberSprung came when Spencer Hering lost a wood-sprung dance floor to a sprinkler system malfunction. Afterwards, Hurricanes Harvey, Irma, and Maria destroyed a plethora of sprung floors in Texas, Florida, and Puerto Rico in the summer of 2017, highlighting the need for a flood-proof flooring system. Spencer had built dance floors before, and got to thinking there had to be a better way.

On his final day as a professional dancer, Spencer brought the idea to product designer Micah Reyes, whose background is composites design through aerospace and yacht design. They quickly realized the potential of composites like fiberglass to increase lifetime, improve consistency, and improve the quality of sprung floors. That is when research and development for FiberSprung began.

FiberSprung uses high performance, long lasting composites in place of the wood inside basket-weave sprung floors. Using structural engineering, our floors match the bend characteristics of a traditional sprung floor — and we developed and patented an elastomer and aluminum fitting method that makes the fiberglass lattice modular and reusable.
